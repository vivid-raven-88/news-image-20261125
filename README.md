A one-page newspaper front page summing up the world's news of
Wednesday 25 November 2026, drawn with Python and PIL: a masthead naming
the day, then six story panels — tankers on fire in the Strait of Hormuz,
the Huawei conviction in a US court, the plunge in Asian tech stocks,
all hostages freed in the Cartagena supermarket siege, Pakistan's first
astronaut reaching orbit aboard China's Shenzhou-24, and the leaked
figure of nearly 500,000 Russian soldiers killed in Ukraine since 2022.

`main.ts` copies the shipped `entry.png` to the output directory and
verifies the copy is byte-identical.
